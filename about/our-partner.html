Our Partners & Benefactors

Celebrating the verified government institutions, international humanitarian bodies, civic clubs, and community supporters who watered the mustard seed since 1963.

Verified Institutional & Historic Partners

Organisations That Built Our Campus & Programs

Documented from the school’s 40th and 50th Anniversary official historical records. Organisational logos are intentionally omitted unless explicitly authorised.

State & Federal Government

Oyo State Government & Ministries

Longstanding institutional partner supporting teacher salaries, boarding feeding subsidies, and public special-education oversight through the Oyo State Ministry of Education and Ministry of Women Affairs & Social Welfare.

Land & Faith Foundation

Anglican Diocese of Ibadan

Responded to the appeal of the World Council of Churches by donating permanent campus land at Ijokodo, establishing the enduring home of Ibadan School for the Deaf.

Global Ecumenical Support

World Council of Churches & Christian Partners

Alongside the Christian Council of Churches in Nigeria, Christian Aid (London), Church of Canada, and Bread for the World (Germany), provided pivotal early funding for school structures and campus growth.

Civic & Service Clubs

Rotary International & Ibadan Rotary Clubs

Rotary Club of Ibadan built a boys’ dormitory; Rotary Club of Eleyele donated a multi-purpose hall; Rotary International District 9130 built four computer-training classrooms; and Rotary Club of Agodi donated the school’s first bus in December 1981.

International Deaf Education

Commonwealth Society for the Deaf (Britain) & Nigerian Youth Trust

The Nigerian Youth Trust built the school’s Audiology Clinic, while the Commonwealth Society for the Deaf helped train Late Mr. Soji Akinbola and supplied hearing-aid testing and repair equipment.

International Children’s Aid

Netherlands Children Used Stamps Group

Nederlands Comité voor Kinderpostzegels donated classroom and boys’ dormitory blocks that expanded learning capacity for deaf pupils at Ijokodo.

Federal Support

Federal Government of Nigeria

Constructed a boys’ dormitory on campus and responded to advocacy from school founders and educators to establish supportive deaf services in Nigerian higher education.

Academic Collaboration

Department of Special Education, University of Ibadan

Academic partner and donor closely linked to the school’s legacy in developing specialised deaf-education teacher training in Nigeria.

Early Humanitarian Societies

YWCA, YMCA & Friends of the Deaf School Society

Together with the American Women’s Group and Nigerian community coordinators led by Prof. Bolanle Awe, raised essential funds for early classroom and girls’ dormitory construction.
