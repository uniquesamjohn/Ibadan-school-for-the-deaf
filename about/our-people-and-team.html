Founding Legacy

Founders of Ibadan School for the Deaf

Verified from the school’s official 40th and 50th Anniversary institutional records.

Founder & First Headmistress (1963 – 1984)

Late Chief (Mrs.) Omodele Adelaide Oyesola, M.O.N., J.P.

Trained at United Missionary College, Oke Ado, and Gallaudet College (now Gallaudet University, Washington D.C.). Established the school in 1963, authored sign-language instructional resources, helped pioneer special education teacher training in Nigeria, and was honoured as M.O.N. and Iya Abiye of Ibadanland in 1973.

Co-Founder · Jaguna of Freeman Methodist Cathedral

Late Chief Jacob Oluseye Oyesola

Co-founder of Ibadan School for the Deaf alongside his wife Chief (Mrs.) A.O. Oyesola. Remembered by alumni, the Board of Governors, and the Oyesola family for unwavering partnership, charitable service, and stewardship in nurturing the school from its 1963 inception.
