Admissions

Welcoming deaf and hearing-impaired children into a supportive, specialist learning community in Ijokodo, Ibadan.

Welcome Parents & Guardians

Every Deaf Child Deserves the Opportunity to Thrive

Parents and guardians are the most important partners in assessing and educating a child with hearing impairment. At Ibadan School for the Deaf, we encourage families to bring their deaf children forward early so they can access specialist teaching, sign language, speech support, and holistic rehabilitation.

Whether your child is beginning early learning at the Pre-Primary stage, transferring from a mainstream school into Primary or Secondary education, or seeking vocational skills with boarding care, our admissions desk is ready to guide your family with dignity and clarity.

Verified Referral & Early Intervention Guidance

  • Children showing signs of hearing difficulty should receive prompt medical examination and assessment.
  • Medical personnel and families commonly refer assessed children to the school for appropriate educational placement.
  • Early intervention promotes stronger language acquisition, literacy, and rehabilitation outcomes.
  • Both day and boarding options are supported depending on placement and availability.
How to Apply

Step-by-Step Admissions Guide

A clear, parent-friendly path from first enquiry to classroom placement.

Step 01

Initial Enquiry

Complete our online Enroll a Child enquiry form, call +234-803-585-1464 or +234-806-006-0642, or visit the school office at Polytechnic Road, Ijokodo Junction, Ibadan.

Step 02

Medical & Hearing Check

Where not yet completed, arrange a medical/audiological evaluation so educators understand the child’s communication and learning needs.

Step 03

Campus Visit & Placement

Meet with the school leadership at Ijokodo for an assessment and guidance on the right stage (Pre-Primary, Primary, Secondary, or Vocational).

Step 04

Official Registration

Confirm required documentation, boarding or day arrangements, and resumption details directly with the school administration.
