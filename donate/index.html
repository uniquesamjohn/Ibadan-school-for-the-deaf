Why Your Support Matters

Continuing a Six-Decade Legacy of Service

Ibadan School for the Deaf began in 1963 as a charity and venture of faith with four pupils. Over the decades, collaboration between Oyo State Government and well-meaning supporters built classrooms, dormitories, a multi-purpose hall, computer-training rooms, and an Audiology Clinic.

As noted by the school’s Board of Governors and 50th Anniversary Planning Committee, equipping hearing-impaired pupils for the 21st century requires ongoing support from every sector of society — both through financial sponsorship of indigent students and through direct infrastructure or equipment donations.
