Serving Deaf Learners

Dedicated Educators & Support Staff Make the Difference

For over six decades, Ibadan School for the Deaf has relied on committed special-education teachers, sign-language educators, speech and audiology personnel, vocational instructors, and residential care staff working in partnership with Oyo State education authorities and the Board of Governors.

Our school also shares a deep historical connection with Nigeria’s specialist teacher-training institutions, including the Department of Special Education at the University of Ibadan and the Federal College of Education (Special), Oyo.
