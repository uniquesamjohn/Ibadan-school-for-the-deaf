Achievements & Institutional Milestones

Verified accomplishments spanning over sixty years of pioneering deaf education, advocacy, and alumni impact in Nigeria.

Verified Milestones

Six Decades of Impact

Every milestone listed below is directly verified from the school’s official 40th and 50th Anniversary documentation.

Pioneer Deaf School in Nigeria (Est. 1963)

Grew from four pupils in a borrowed Ososami Road garage into a landmark permanent residential and day institution at Ijokodo serving thousands of deaf learners over more than six decades.

Catalyst for University of Ibadan Special Education

Founder Chief (Mrs.) A.O. Oyesola collaborated with Prof. S.A. Bakre, P.O. Mba, Rev. M.A. Awe, Alhaji Daodu, Mr. Gbegbin, Dr. Adeniran, and Mr. B.O. Afolabi to launch deaf-education teacher training at the University of Ibadan, leading to the Department of Special Education.

Advocacy for UNILORIN & FCE (Special) Oyo

Contributed during the formative Governing Council years of Federal College of Education (Special), Oyo, and advocated successfully with the Federal Ministry of Education for supportive deaf services at the University of Ilorin.

National Honours (M.O.N., 1973)

The Federal Government of Nigeria conferred Member of the Order of the Niger (M.O.N.) on Founder Chief (Mrs.) A.O. Oyesola in 1973, alongside her chieftaincy honour as Iya Abiye of Ibadanland and appointment as Justice of the Peace.

Sign Language Publications & Ministry

Produced sign-language instructional books supporting teachers and learners, and helped introduce sign language training at Emmanuel College of Theology to foster wider societal inclusion.

Alumni Excellence Across Professions

Former students serve across Nigeria and abroad as higher-institution lecturers (such as Mr. Bamidele Wasiu Raji at FCE Special Oyo), teachers, engineers, administrators, civil servants, and independent business owners.

Documented Facility Milestones

Key Facilities Built With Partners

  • Permanent campus land at Ijokodo donated by the Anglican Diocese of Ibadan
  • Audiology Clinic constructed by the Nigerian Youth Trust and equipped with support from the Commonwealth Society for the Deaf (Britain)
  • Boys’ and girls’ dormitories funded by Nigerian community coordinators (Prof. Bolanle Awe), Netherlands Children Used Stamps Group, Rotary Club of Ibadan, and the Federal Government of Nigeria
  • Multi-purpose hall donated by Rotary Club of Eleyele
  • Four computer-training classrooms built by Rotary International District 9130
  • First school bus donated in December 1981 by Rotary Club of Agodi (and a subsequent school bus donated by Ever Increasing Joy Ministries)
  • High-capacity generator donated by Military Governor Major General David Jemibewon
