Exodus chapter four verse eleven (Exodus 4:11) makes it clear that it is “the Lord that made the deaf just as He created other individuals”. Therefore, any hearing-impaired person should not be given opportunity like other persons in the family and society. Deaf children are not dumb, mute or mentally deficient. They may experience difficulties in spoken and written languages as a result of their hearing problems. Hearing impairment as currently in use is concerned with personality, communication and language. Hearing impairment is ... extra-ordinary measures applicable to aspects of living, feeling and thinking for their education. Those people with hearing impairment differ from the deaf because of the degree and type of hearing loss. Some people with hearing impairment may either become deaf or have partial hearing impairment, others have demonstrated over-protection to the extent that other children could perform below children of their age. There is need to dis-abuse the minds of those who still believe that hearing problem is due to sins or crimes committed against gods which as a result into neglect and abandonment.

Observable signs of hearing impairment

Today, advances in medical research and public education interventions have reduced the handicapping conditions of children. Once we can recognize the observable signs of hearing difficulties early enough, some can be treated or cured. Some noticeable signs that can indicate that a child is not hearing normally include the following:

  1. Confusing or refusing verbal instruction
  2. Rubbing the ears frequently
  3. Turning the head in one direction to locate sound
  4. Complaining of ringing in the ear
  5. Discharge from the ears
  6. Requesting for repetition of what has been said to him or her
  7. Watching the lips
  8. Complaining about a normal sound that it is too low or inaudible
  9. Abnormal speech
  10. Avoiding situations that require him or her to speak
  11. Abnormal dizziness

When a child manifests one or a combination of these symptoms, he should be referred for immediate medical examination and treatment. Medical personnel know exactly what to do with the child after treatment. In most cases, such children are referred to the school where appropriate education would be provided. Suffice to say that early intervention promotes better education and rehabilitation for children with hearing problems.

CAUSES OF HEARING IMPAIRMENT

There are a number of causes of hearing impairment. These include:

  1. Drugs taken by pregnant women e.g. quinine
  2. Blood incompatibility
  3. Sexually transmitted diseases
  4. Severe slap to the ear
  5. Noise and blasts
  6. Heredity
  7. Development of abnormalities during pregnancy
  8. Mumps, Influenza, Meningitis, and other similar bacterial infections like German Measles
  9. Vitamin deficiency
  10. Birth injuries
  11. Occupational hearing loss - factory and construction site noise, where heavy machine is being used, military blasts, etc
  12. Aging
  13. Loud musical sound
  14. Tinnitus (ringing in the ears)

PREVENTION OF HEARING IMPAIRMENT

  1. Pregnant mothers should avoid self medication
  2. Avoid sexually transmitted disease
  3. Do not expose your ear to loud music and blasts. Use ear plugs or protectors
  4. Avoid marriage between people with congenital hearing loss
  5. Beware of excessive wax inside the ear
  6. Keep your ear dry
  7. Avoid the use of swabs and other objects
  8. Pre-natal and post-natal immunization the mother receives before birth and after birth, the child receives both

PARENT RESPONSIBILITY

Parents should note that they are the most important resources in accessing and educating a child. At the first contact the child has, parents should endeavour to provide the needed assistance to their wards. Besides, parents should also note that deaf people are not so aggressive as they think. When they show anger, it is simply as a result of lack of understanding their needs due to communication barriers. Parents can confirm the facts that new born babies can hear when they are talking when they turn their head to look in their direction. It is the same with older children. For example, musical toys that are pleasing to them. However, mechanical sound makers should not replace normal rhythmic speech and music. Parents and many care givers have many natural opportunities to talk to babies right from birth during feeding, bathing, dressing, and other occasions that can be used to express love and praise. When a child is called by his or her name, he or she will always respond positively. Some parents do not call their children by name once they discover that they have hearing problems. The hearing impaired child should be integrated into the family activities. Parents should also try to learn sign language to communicate with their children. They can look for the service of a sign language interpreter for help at home most especially on communication.

CONCLUSION

Hearing impairment is a condition for which there are possible solutions. Early intervention, exposure to education and programme of rehabilitation promote the wellbeing of the deaf in our society.
