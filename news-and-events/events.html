Documented History

Notable Historical School Occasions

Verified landmark occasions preserved in the school’s official anniversary archives.

1967 Ijokodo Campus, Ibadan

Permanent Site Land Donation & Foundation Laying

Establishment of the school’s permanent campus at Ijokodo following the Anglican Diocese of Ibadan land donation and community fundraising for the first classrooms.

State Visit Ijokodo Campus, Ibadan

Official Visit by Oyo State Military Governor

Historic campus visit by Major General David Jemibewon, who inspected classes, greeted students, and donated a major power generator to support the school community.

40th & 50th Anniversaries Ibadan, Oyo State

Golden Jubilee (50th Anniversary) Celebrations

Commemorated half a century since the 1963 founding, bringing together the Board of Governors, Oyo State leadership, alumni sets, parents, and international well-wishers.
