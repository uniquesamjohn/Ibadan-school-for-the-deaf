CHAIRMAN'S OPENING SPEECH

I am highly delighted to congratulate you on the celebration of the 60th Anniversary of the founding of the Ibadan School for the Deaf.

My connection with the founders of the school and the entire Oyesola family dated back to 1960. I first met Rev (Dr) J. O. Oyesola when he came to visit his twin brother S. O. Oyesola who happens to be my classmate in college. Since then I and J. O. Oyesola have been friends ever since.

Ibadan School for the Deaf is the second school for the deaf established in Nigeria. The first one called the Federal School for the Deaf in Lagos was established in 1960. The name was later changed to Wesley School for the Deaf, Surulere, Lagos.

Ibadan School for the Deaf founded by Late Chief Mrs Oyesola became distinct among all the deaf schools established in Nigeria.

In 1960, I became a member and a staff for the deaf school in Lagos.

Later the same year, I was awarded with commonwealth scholarship to study Audiology and Deaf Education in Grays Inn Road Hospital in London. After my training I came back to Nigeria to work at the Wesley School for the Deaf, Surulere, Lagos as Audiology Technician. It was around that time Late Chief Mrs Oyesola was training in this Great school in Gallaudet College, Washington DC. By the time we both finished training she established this Great School for the Deaf in Ibadan. When I was working in Lagos she was working at Wesley School for the Deaf in Lagos until 1968 when I came to work in Ibadan at the A.K.T. Chairman and I was asked to see the Screening of the students entering the School. When I was working in Lagos she was working at Wesley School for the Deaf in Lagos until 1968 when I came to work in Ibadan at the A.K.T. Chairman and I was asked to see the Screening of the students entering the School.

Later the society for the deaf in Lagos in which I belong to organized a conference for the School for the Deaf in West and Central Africa. The meeting took place at the Ibadan School for the Deaf Ground. People came from the Old Western State and other states in Nigeria. At that Meeting, the nature of the society was changed from the Society for the Care of the Deaf to National Advisory Council for the Deaf (N.A.C.D). Officers were appointed in that place which were Late Alhaji S. A. Daudu, Late Chief Mrs Oyesola (Vice Chairman) and I, M. A. Awe was elected as National Secretary of N.A.C.D with late Dr P.O. Mba as a Member of the Board for Deaf Schools for the Deaf. Oyo and I with Late Dr Mba travelled to the then 19 states of Federation to encourage Deaf Schools for the Deaf.

One particular achievement of the Ibadan School for the Deaf is that the Methodist Grammar school, Bodija created room for Deaf Secondary School students, that was when I was Chairman of the Parents Teachers Association (P.T.A) of the Grammar School. I congratulate the Board of the School for the Deaf and pray that the memory of the late founders of the School, Late Chief Mrs Oyesola and Late Chief J. O. Oyesola continue to remain green.

Rev (Dr) M. A. Awe

Former General Secretary of the National Council of the Deaf in Nigeria

ADDRESS BY THE MATRON

60 HEARTY CHEERS TO IBADAN SCHOOL FOR THE DEAF

Mrs Temitope Adewole-Oyesola

To the glory of God, I am using this great opportunity to congratulate Ibadan School for the Deaf on this landmark anniversary and also welcome the Chairman of the occasion, the Royal Father of the day, Royal Father of the day, Chairman Board of Trustees, Chairman and members Management Board, Chairman and members Anniversary Planning Committee, distinguished guests, the Principal, the Headmistress, all teachers, special domestic staff and my lovely students.

Over five decades ago, I was born into the community of the hearing impaired by Chief Jacob Oluseye and Chief (Mrs) Adelaide Omidele Oyesola (both of blessed memory), the founders of Ibadan School for the Deaf. I grew up with most of these children who had passed through the School from inception. It was my Secondary School and higher education that took me out of the beautiful community for a while but I used to return during the holidays. To the glory of God, I started my educational career at Ibadan School for the Deaf (Staff School), Ibadan followed by Fiwasaye Grammar School, Akure, Ondo State for my secondary education. I had National Diploma in Catering, Hotel and Hostel Management in the then Ondo State Polytechnic, Owo, now Rufus Giwa Polytechnic, Owo, Ondo State. I also had professional examination in City and Guilds Niche... and London City Guild (Catering). Various trainings have been given to me on different occasions by the United Nations Development Programme / International Labour Organization (UNDP / ILO) as an Officer-in-Training for the disabled. In the year 2000, I became a Rotary Centennial President. Before the retirement of my mother as the warden of the Home, I became a helper, supporting her in the hostel management. At that time, I was running my businesses titled "Oluwaloni 3Hers Catering, Rentals and Decoration" and "Loni Farms". It was in 1999 that I was made the substantive matron of the School, a position I occupy till date.

This 60th School Anniversary gives me the opportunity to showcase the achievements of the school and our Students having some as professors, lecturers, civil servants in various ministries, artisans, businessmen and women, engineers, architects, pharmacists, lecturers, craftsmen and successfully married men and women with grandchildren. These children have been possible without the support of our parents, government and the good parents. I appreciate your advice, generous visitations and the likes that have become encouragement to us as we go about our duties in the midst of our students. Thank you for your support.

Furthermore, permit me to inform you that the school needs help in the following areas: qualified teachers, rehabilitation of classrooms, interpreters, school bus, event center for generation of funds, maintenance of facilities in the School, solar lights, children sponsorship, completion of perimeter fencing, dining chairs and tables, hostel painting, photocopy machine, cooking utensils, generator and financial support. If these needs are met, our ... the school a boost. Therefore, it is our clarion call to all ... here present and those represented by proxy ... assistance that will go a long way in giving our children ... As we celebrate the 60th Anniversary existence of Ibadan School for the Deaf, I pray that the School will continue to move forward, our students will continue to excel in life and their various pursuits, the parents, all that belong to this great family will not grow weary and the Lord will bless their endeavours while we ... labours of our esteemed donors, ... distinguished guests will be greatly rewarded. May the Lord grant you journey mercies to your destinations. Thank you and God bless.

MRS TEMITOPE ADEWOLE-OYESOLA

A daughter of the founders and serving matron of the school who has served meritoriously for twenty-five years
